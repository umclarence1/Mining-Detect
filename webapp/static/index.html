About the data

Dry-season (Nov–Feb) median composites of Copernicus Sentinel-2, cloud-masked with Cloud Score+. U-Net (ResNet-18, 12 bands) trained on 900 tiles from the Ankobra, Pra and Offin basins; Tarkwa held out. The model detects mining disturbance, not legality: the industrial footprint (Maus et al. 2022) stands in for licence boundaries. Validation points were interpreted visually by an AI assistant, without field checks.
